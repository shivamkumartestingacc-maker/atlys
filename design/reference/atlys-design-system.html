Atlys · design system v1 · light

One system, six new screens

Foundations and components pulled from the Atlys app, then used to design screens that don't exist in the app today: a multi-traveller trip hub, a document fix, a pre-submit check, approval, a day-before checklist and the document vault.

Destination photos, orbs and the perk card are cropped from the Atlys recordings. Names, prices, dates and odds are sample content.
